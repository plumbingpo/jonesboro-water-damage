A dry house is not yet a finished house. Once the moisture readings are back to normal, there are still cut walls, missing flooring and bare subfloor to deal with. Rebuilding all of that is what water damage repair means. In Jonesboro, we put back the materials that were lost and match them to the originals, turning a work zone into a normal room again.

Repair work covers a wide range. Smaller projects might involve only paint, fresh baseboard and a short section of drywall. At the bigger end, expect doors, trim, cabinet boxes, hardwood or laminate flooring, subfloor, full drywall sheets and insulation. Our aim is a like-for-like replacement, and when a true match cannot be found we say so directly and let you decide.

We do not close anything in while it is still damp. Ahead of installing anything new, our team meters the framing and subfloor to verify normal moisture. Water Damage Repair Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Ask for final moisture readings before any contractor closes up a wall or floor.
  • Photograph damaged materials before they are removed in case your insurer asks later.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Laundry rooms, bathrooms and kitchens should be rebuilt with moisture resistant wallboard and proper backer board.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Ceiling drywall is sagging, cracked or crumbling after a leak from above.
  • Laminate or vinyl plank edges are swollen, curled or separating at the seams.
  • Flooring flexes more than it should in the spot that was flooded.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • Drywall was cut out during drying and the wall cavities are still open.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.

What to Expect From Our Water Damage Repair

We follow a proven process to ensure every job is completed to the highest standards. Here's how we handle water damage repair from start to finish:

1

Confirm It Is Dry

Final moisture readings are taken on the subfloor, framing and any drywall left in place. Reconstruction only begins once those numbers are at normal levels for the material, so nothing damp gets sealed in.

2

Put the Scope in Writing

You receive a room by room list of what will be replaced, the materials we plan to use and the expected schedule. If a claim is open, we line the scope up with the adjuster.

3

Reconstruct and Install

We refill open cavities with insulation, hang, tape and finish drywall, and repair the subfloor. Flooring, cabinets and trim are then installed in the order that protects the finished surfaces.

4

Finish and Walk Through

We match texture, prime and paint, and reinstall doors, baseboards and hardware. Together we then walk through each room, and every listed item is corrected before the job is closed.
